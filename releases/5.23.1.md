# 5.23.1

Fixes an increment acceptance that QFN Games could not pass on 2026-11-12. Technical review of the increment approved
every acceptance criterion (both criteria chunks approved, and the whole-candidate consistency check found nothing
blocking), but the outcome was `changes_requested` with 21 findings. The board showed the consistency summary, "No
additional blocking consistency findings…", as the block reason.

## Cause

A candidate whose review input exceeds 160,000 characters is first read in bounded parts
(`AggregateSourceReview`). Each part's inspection is note-taking for the later review and grants no approval, but
`DeliveryScopeReview` appended every inspection finding to the verdict and forced `approved = false`. The inspections
had filled `findings` with observations ("dependency pin evidence is consistent"), optional robustness ideas, and a
false "missing file" for scripts that were in another part. The reviewers who saw the whole candidate, and those notes,
had no way to rule them out.

## Inspection defects are ruled on, not appended

- Source inspection reports now return `notes` (evidence for the later review) and `defects` (faults the supplied parts
  prove on their own) separately, and receive `candidateFiles`, the full file list, so a file in another part is never
  reported as missing. `AggregateSourceReview.Policy` is `aggregate-source-chunks-2026-11-12.v2`; inspections retained
  under the old policy are not reused.
- When inspections report defects, a final step (`InspectionDefectAdjudication`) rules on each one by id against the
  complete reference, the acceptance criteria and the verdict. A genuine or uncertain defect becomes a blocking finding.
  A dismissal needs a concrete reason. A response that skips, repeats or invents an id is corrected like any other
  schema error, so no defect disappears silently.
- Every ruling, including dismissals and their reasons, is kept in the outcome output under
  `sourceInspectionRulings`. Platforms ignore the extra property.
- When no inspection reports a defect, no extra model call is made.

## A rejection says what blocks

`DeliveryScopeReview` now starts a rejected outcome's summary with
`Changes requested (N blocking findings): <first finding>`, followed by the reviewer's summary (at most 4,000
characters). The platform shows this summary as the stage's block reason.

## Upgrading

Agents that call `DeliveryScopeReview` (Technical Director, Producer) pick this up by referencing 5.23.1. To re-run a
review that was rejected this way, retry the blocked stage after the agents are updated.
