Add reusable planning-advice receipt validation, current staffing/resource context and explicit decision-replacement traversal. Requester finalization consumes the expected specialist's complete answer without entering a different workflow or generating another model response. Retained answers remain verifiable after an older requester routing failure; current board ownership and exact accepted sources still apply.

PlanningResourceContext distinguishes proposals, staffing approvals, actual roster membership and verified resource readiness. Its substantive fingerprint supports reassessment after changed facts without treating a brief's authority disclaimer as a prohibition. DecisionContinuations follows explicit replacements, rejects cycles and cross-project references, and has no fixed replacement-depth rule.

Requires WorkManagement.Contracts 5.9.0. No new capability or execution authority is granted.

Validation: regression suites and package-consumer verification are recorded in the platform's planning-context recovery document.
